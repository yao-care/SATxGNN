## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available (DrugBank MOA field returned a data gap). Based on known information drawn from the literature evidence in this pack, oxaliplatin is part of the platinum-based chemotherapy class — specifically a third-generation cisplatin analogue with a DACH (1,2-diaminocyclohexane) carrier ligand (PMID 10936465). Its efficacy in colorectal cancer, particularly in combination with 5-FU, has been proven in controlled Phase 3 trials, and mechanistically it may be applicable to malignant pleural mesothelioma (MPM).

Platinum compounds (cisplatin/carboplatin) are already the chemotherapy backbone of MPM, typically combined with pemetrexed as standard of care. Oxaliplatin is pharmacologically related and has been explored as an alternative platinum agent — with a comparatively lower renal- and oto-toxicity profile — in several completed Phase 2 combination trials with gemcitabine and raltitrexed. These trials demonstrate biological activity of oxaliplatin-based regimens in MPM, even though oxaliplatin has not been adopted as standard therapy for this indication.

The shared DNA-crosslinking mechanism across platinum agents provides a plausible pharmacological bridge between the two indications. However, results across the oxaliplatin+raltitrexed trials are mixed (one phase II study reported activity, a subsequent second-line study reported no objective responses — PMID 15893013), so the evidence supports further clinical evaluation rather than a settled therapeutic role.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 2 trials — including one directly testing oxaliplatin + gemcitabine in mesothelioma (NCT00859469) and multiple raltitrexed/oxaliplatin combination studies — show biological activity, but results are mixed (a subsequent second-line trial reported no responses), and no Phase 3 evidence exists. Critically, this candidate has a **Blocking** data gap: the local safety label (warnings/contraindications) is unavailable, which by definition prevents entry into the S1 safety review stage, and the drug is not currently marketed in Saudi Arabia.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- SFDA-equivalent package insert (warnings, contraindications) to clear the S1 safety gate
- Confirmed mechanism of action data via DrugBank API

- Update evidence review once ongoing/unknown-status trials (NCT00996385, NCT03210298) report results
